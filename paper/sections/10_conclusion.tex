\section{Conclusion}
Before asking whether a trained forecaster behaves like population Bayes, like a library posterior or like a context-only
estimator, one should ask whether those references predict differently on the data at hand. For scalar autoregressive
systems they mostly do not: population knowledge is worth a few hundredths of a nat and only at very short contexts,
observation noise erases even that, and finite libraries are indistinguishable from continuous priors on natural evaluation
data. The window opens when the noise scale is unknown, which is where we would place a neural study. We offer the
references, the maps and the pre-registration machinery as a precondition for behavioural claims about in-context learners
of dynamical systems.
