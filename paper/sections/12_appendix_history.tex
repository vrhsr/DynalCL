\section{The pre-registered studies that preceded the maps}\label{app:history}
Three pre-registered studies in the scalar families came before the reference maps of the main text. Each used the same
margin $\delta=\DeltaNats$ nats but its own decision rule; their outcomes are reported here as they happened.

\paragraph{Stage~0 (first separability map).} The first map classified each regime by an interval rule against a frontier of
context-only estimators (flat-prior Bayes and least-squares plug-ins) rather than the stability-only Bayes reference of the
main text. It found \SzeroAllPairs of \SzeroCells regimes in which all three strategy pairs were separable, and on in-library
systems it found the library and continuous posteriors equivalent within $\delta$ in all \SzeroElibEquiv cells. Its
reference validation is summarised in Section~\ref{sec:val}.

\paragraph{\sHalf (population advantage).} Pre-registered with fresh seeds and two replicates that had to agree, with intervals
of $\pm$\SfiveIntervalSE standard errors, it compared population Bayes with the strongest context-only estimator. It confirmed a
population-specific advantage at one-step but not at five-step horizons and returned the pre-registered decision REVISE. A
library of two systems was distinguishable from the continuous prior on held-out systems in \LibSfiveDist of \LibSfiveCells
confirmatory cells. These results led to the stability-only Bayes comparator of the main text.

\paragraph{\sSix (observation noise, long contexts).} Pre-registered and frozen behind an authorization gate, it asked whether
population knowledge gives a meaningful advantage at long contexts under additive observation noise, using a studentised
max-$|t|$ bootstrap over all contrasts. Every long-context gain had an adjusted upper bound below $\delta$ in both replicates,
and the pre-registered verdict was STOP. The noisy-family map of the main text extends this to short contexts.

\section{Provenance of the maps and analyses}\label{app:provenance}
In this paper ``pre-registered'' refers only to the studies of Appendix~\ref{app:history} and Appendix~\ref{sec:pilot}, which
were frozen with fixed decision rules and confirmatory seeds. The maps and analyses of the main text are reference computations:
for each, the specification (grid, sample sizes, rules, seed role) was committed to the repository before its seed was first
used, and the disclosure log records what had been seen at that time. Their designs were chosen after earlier results, as
Table~\ref{tab:provenance} lists; they are therefore confirmatory only in the sense that no specification was changed after
its own results were seen.
