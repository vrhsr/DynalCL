\section{Validation of the references}\label{sec:val}
A wrong reference silently invalidates every later classification, so each reference was validated before it was used,
with tolerances fixed in advance and every failure disclosed.

\paragraph{Known noise scale.} The matched reference passed an optimality check (no context-only or library reference beats
it in expectation on its own systems) with no violation in \SzeroTone paired comparisons, and a calibration check (PIT,
coverage, simulation-based calibration \citep{talts2018}) with no failing cell out of \SzeroTtwoCells (smallest $p$-value
\SzeroTtwoMinP). The \sHalf re-run of these checks, with fresh seeds, found no violation in \SfiveToneComparisons comparisons
and no failing cell out of \SfiveTtwoCells. Where an integral over $a$ is computed by quadrature it is numerically
approximated with verified convergence against a pre-set target of $10^{-6}$ nats: the largest quadrature error in the
\sHalf study was \SfiveQuadErr nats, and node doubling changed log scores by at most \FoneMapNodeDoubling nats over
\FoneMapChecks checks in the F1 map and \FthreeNodeDoubling nats in the F3 map.

\paragraph{Unknown noise scale (F1).} The noise variance is integrated in closed form and $a$ by quadrature; closed forms were
checked against adaptive one-dimensional integration, the predictive against a dense two-dimensional grid, and a
known-scale limit was confirmed. At the scale of the first study the F1 references passed all \FoneValTests calibration,
coverage, simulation-based-calibration and optimality tests at family level \FamilyAlpha (Bonferroni). A mutation matrix
injected \FoneMutations plausible bugs (wrong prior, off-by-one likelihood, leaked target, unmatched reference, wrong
truncation, wrong noise integration); every one was detected and the clean code passed. One honest weakness: the wrong
truncation bound is caught only by the structural prior-matching guard, not by any statistical check, because it moves
little prior mass.

\paragraph{Bivariate VAR(1) (F2).} The conjugate update, the Student-$t$ predictives with and without the noise covariance
integrated, and the library mixture match closed forms or \texttt{scipy} to $10^{-10}$; the stability-truncated predictive
matches nested quadrature in the one-dimensional reduction, agrees with an independent prior-sampling estimator and with an
independent interval-integration estimator, and its Monte Carlo error is reported per item. Calibration, optimality and
mutation tests for F2 are not yet run (Section~\ref{sec:limits}).

\paragraph{Noisy observations (F3).} The Kalman-likelihood references were frozen and validated in the \sSix study (their own
pre-registered critical checks all passed); the F3 map additionally checked node doubling, a library predictor against an
independently written scalar Kalman filter, and agreement of its noise-free column with the known-scale map.
