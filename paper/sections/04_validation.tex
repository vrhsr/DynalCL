\section{Validation of the references}\label{sec:val}
A wrong reference silently invalidates every later classification, so each reference was validated before it was used,
with tolerances fixed in advance and every failure disclosed. The statistical checks below are deliberately conservative
(Bonferroni-corrected at family level, with correspondingly strict per-test thresholds), so they detect gross errors rather
than certify small ones; the mutation tests show which injected errors they do detect.

\paragraph{Known noise scale.} The matched reference passed an optimality check (no context-only or library reference beats
it in expectation on its own systems) with no violation in \SzeroTone paired comparisons, and a calibration check (PIT,
coverage, simulation-based calibration \citep{talts2018}) with no failing cell out of \SzeroTtwoCells (smallest $p$-value
\SzeroTtwoMinP). The \sHalf re-run, with fresh seeds, found no violation in \SfiveToneComparisons comparisons and no failing
cell out of \SfiveTtwoCells. Where an integral over $a$ is computed by quadrature it is numerically approximated with
verified convergence against a pre-set target of $10^{-6}$ nats: the largest quadrature error in the \sHalf study was
\SfiveQuadErr nats, and node doubling changed log scores by at most \FoneMapNodeDoubling nats over \FoneMapChecks checks in
the F1 map and \FthreeNodeDoubling nats in the F3 map.

\paragraph{Unknown noise scale (F1).} The noise variance is integrated in closed form and $a$ by quadrature; closed forms were
checked against adaptive one-dimensional integration, the predictive against a dense two-dimensional grid, and a
known-scale limit was confirmed. The F1 references passed all \FoneValTests calibration, coverage,
simulation-based-calibration and optimality tests at family level \FamilyAlpha (Bonferroni). A mutation matrix injected
\FoneMutations plausible bugs (wrong prior, off-by-one likelihood, leaked target, unmatched reference, wrong truncation,
wrong noise integration); every one was detected and the clean code passed. The wrong truncation bound is caught only by
the structural prior-matching guard, not by any statistical check, because it moves little prior mass.

\paragraph{Noisy observations (F3).} The Kalman-likelihood references were frozen and validated in the \sSix study, whose
mutation matrix killed all \SsixMutations injected bugs with a clean baseline; the F3 map additionally checked node
doubling, a library predictor against an independently written scalar Kalman filter, and its noise-free column against the
known-scale map on three cells.

\paragraph{Bivariate VAR(1) (F2).} The F2 references are implemented and their tests pass locally; their calibration,
optimality and mutation results are not yet available and nothing in this paper depends on them.
